OPERATIONS OPTIMIZATION

SBM Top-Off Application

Supporting uniform beam injection and machine efficiency through controls automation and data-informed optimization.

Operational Goal

Top-off injection must replenish stored beam while maintaining stable operating conditions for users. The application supported more uniform injection behavior and reduced the operational burden of maintaining the desired fill pattern.

Controls and Optimization

The work combined accelerator controls integration with machine-learning techniques used to evaluate and improve operating behavior. The application translated operational objectives into coordinated settings and feedback that operators could supervise.

Impact

The result supported improved injection consistency and machine efficiency while preserving operator visibility into system state.
