How the pilot could work

Small, local, and designed for success

01 · Describe yourselfAnswer practical questions about communication, planning, sensory preferences, routine, energy, and support needs.
02 · Compare thoughtfullySee shared patterns, meaningful differences, and suggested conversation prompts—without clinical labels.
03 · Graduate from the appPause or close your membership when you find a relationship worth exploring. Retention is not the point.

Concept disclaimer: All profiles, traits, values, and compatibility results shown here are fictional placeholders. They are not diagnoses, clinical assessments, or real compatibility findings. The eventual product would require informed consent, transparent scoring, privacy controls, and professional review before real matching begins.
